01The Idea

Close to the source, close to the fire.

Marrow is a forty-seat room built around one wood-burning hearth. Chef Nora Vance opened it in 2017 with a simple argument: the Pacific Northwest already grows everything worth eating — all a kitchen owes it is patience, and dry oak.

So that's the whole trick. Leeks buried in embers overnight. Trout laid straight over the coals. Butter browned at the edge of the fire, bread baked in it each morning. Everything else came out of the ground today.

One hearth. Forty seats. No shortcuts.

Sourced within 200 miles when the land allows
Oak from the Gorge · No gas in the kitchen
Bread baked in the coals each morning
